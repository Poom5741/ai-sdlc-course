# Quest 19.3: Ethical Decision Framework

**Block**: 19 - Responsible AI | **Difficulty**: 🟡 Medium | **Time**: 25 minutes

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Design an ethical AI decision framework.
- **Ethics by design** — build ethical considerations into the process.

## 💡 Hints

- Cover: stakeholders, fairness, transparency, accountability, escalation, monitoring.
- The framework should be actionable, not just theoretical.
