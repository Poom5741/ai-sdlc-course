# Quest 19.1: AI Bias Auditor

**Block**: 19 - Responsible AI | **Difficulty**: 🟡 Medium | **Time**: 25 minutes

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Compute fairness metrics for AI predictions.
- **Measure fairness** — you can't improve what you don't measure.

## 💡 Hints

- Fairness must be computed BETWEEN groups, not by averaging everything together.
- Demographic parity: difference in positive prediction rates between groups.
- Equalized odds: difference in true positive rates between groups.
