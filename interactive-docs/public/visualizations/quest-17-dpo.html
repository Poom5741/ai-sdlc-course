How DPO works: Instead of training a reward model separately, DPO directly optimizes the policy using chosen vs rejected response pairs. The loss pushes the model to assign higher probability to chosen responses and lower to rejected ones. The margin (Δ) grows as training progresses — larger Δ = stronger preference alignment.
